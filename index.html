What We Offer

Lessons for every level

Whether you're a complete beginner or just need to polish your skills before the test, we have the right programme for you.

🚗

Code 8 — Light Motor

Training for a standard car (manual or automatic). Perfect for first-time drivers wanting their everyday licence. Includes K53 yard work and road test prep.

Most popular licence
🚛

Code 10 — Heavy Motor

For trucks and heavy vehicles without a trailer. Ideal for those looking to enter the transport and logistics industry in South Africa.

Career opportunity
🚚

Code 14 — Extra Heavy

The highest truck licence — covers extra heavy motor vehicles with a trailer (articulated trucks). Opens doors to long-haul and freight driving careers.

Top qualification
